# 🌍 Project Overview

**NRC-OS (National Response Coordination Operating System)** is an AI-powered emergency coordination platform designed to detect, analyze, and manage real-time disaster incidents from multiple data sources.

The platform integrates **AI intelligence, spatial awareness, and operational coordination tools** to provide a unified command center for emergency management.

Instead of fragmented alerts and disconnected systems, NRC-OS creates a **single operational dashboard for real-time situational awareness and rapid response coordination**.

---

# 🎯 Vision

> **One Screen. One Truth. Faster Response.**

NRC-OS aims to transform disaster management systems by enabling **early incident detection, coordinated response, and intelligent decision-making**.

---

# 🚨 Problem Statement

During disasters, information is scattered across multiple sources:

• Emergency alerts arrive late  
• Agencies operate in silos  
• Data is fragmented  
• Early warning signals are missed  
• Decision-makers lack a unified operational view  

This results in **slow response times and avoidable damage**.

---

# 💡 Solution

NRC-OS solves this by building an **AI-assisted emergency response operating system** that:

• Detects emerging incidents using AI and signal analysis  
• Displays real-time incidents on a command dashboard  
• Provides AI-generated decision support  
• Tracks resources and personnel deployment  
• Integrates spatial intelligence using OpenStreetMap  

The platform acts as a **central digital command center for emergency coordination**.

---

# 🧠 Core Innovation

### AI-Powered Real-Time Incident Discovery

NRC-OS uses AI models through **OpenRouter** to analyze signals from multiple sources such as:

• Weather anomalies  
• Satellite fire detections  
• Infrastructure alerts  
• Public reports  
• Telegram signal ingestion  

The AI engine correlates weak signals and generates **candidate incidents with confidence scores**, allowing authorities to detect emergencies earlier.

Human operators always remain **in control of final decisions**.

# 🗺️ Spatial Intelligence

NRC-OS integrates **OpenStreetMap (OSM)** to provide geographic awareness.

This enables the system to analyze:

• Road networks  
• Bridges and infrastructure  
• Building density  
• Hospitals and emergency shelters  
• Evacuation routes  

Spatial intelligence allows AI to understand **real-world geography and infrastructure constraints**.

# 🧭 Platform Overview & Navigation

NRC-OS provides an integrated **Command Center interface**.

### Main Navigation Modules

• **Overview** – System-wide operational dashboard  
• **Incidents** – Active incident tracking and management  
• **Resources** – Monitoring deployed equipment and personnel  
• **Live Map** – Geographic visualization of incidents and resources  
• **AI Advisory / AI Discovery** – AI-generated insights and anomaly detection  
• **SOP Status** – Monitoring execution of Standard Operating Procedures  
• **Live Comms** – Communication channels between response teams  
• **Agencies** – Multi-agency coordination management  
• **API Status** – Monitoring external data source connectivity
